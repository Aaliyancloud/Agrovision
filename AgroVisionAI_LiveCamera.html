Tomato leaf screening

Check the leaf. Catch the blight early.

Photograph a tomato leaf and AgroVision reads its colour pattern in your browser. It tells you whether the leaf looks healthy, shows signs of blight or yellowing, or is not a leaf photo at all.

      AgroVision · Class 11 STEAM Exhibition, Pakistan

      Farming supports millions of livelihoods in Pakistan. As Class 11 students we built this to show how a phone camera and simple code can help growers notice disease sooner.

      This is a learning demonstration, not a diagnosis. Confirm any treatment with a local agriculture extension officer.
